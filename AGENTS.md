## Rules
- **Pehle poochho, phir badlo:** Story / GDD mein koi bada change karne se pehle ROSE se approval lo. Chhota fix bhi bina bataye mat karo.
- **MD, PDF nahi:** Documents MD file mein banao. PDF token bahut kharch karta hai.
- **Ek milestone ek time par:** Ek milestone poora karo, test karo, phir agla shuru karo. Saare systems ek saath mat banao.
- **2D pixel art hi:** 3D ya 2.5D mat banao. Character/tiles 64x64.
- **Canon mat banao:** Guardian naam, Divine Sword design/forge jagah, Arjun ka final look, controls, HP/Mana rules — ye abhi decide nahi hue hain. Inhe invent karke canon mat banao.
- **Placeholder saaf likho:** Placeholder art/code ho to clearly likho ki ye placeholder hai.
- **Test kiye bina complete mat bolo:** Godot mein test nahi hua ho to saaf likho ki tested nahi hai.
- **Kaam ki report:** Har kaam ke baad Inspected / Plan / Changed / Tested / Not tested / Next step likho.
- **Git commit/push:** Sirf tabhi karo jab ROSE khud bole.

## Locked canon (abhi tak)
- Hero Arjun, bhai Devansh (Nishachar Dal ne kidnap kiya), mentor Rishivar
- Nishachar Dal leader Kalanemi (male dark mage, high magic, intelligent, HP recovery)
- Members: Maya (illusions), Bhaisaur (half human/half bull, high physical, less intelligence)
- Final villain Kaalasur
- Biome order: Ice, Wind, Earth/Rock Cave, Fire. Guardians hi bosses hain, naam decide nahi hue.
- Ending A pehle: Seal the Gate, save Devansh. Ending B/C baad mein.
